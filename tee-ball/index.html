For grown-ups

Super Tee Ball teaches the basic rules of baseball in short lessons. Coach Buddy, the dog in the cap, reads every line out loud, so kids who aren't reading yet can play on their own. Each finished lesson earns a gold star.

The lessons

  1. The Field. Home plate, first, second and third base, and the nine positions.
  2. Batter Up. Hit the ball off the tee, drop the bat (never throw it), and run to first base.
  3. Run the Bases. First, second, third, home. Stay on your base until the ball is hit. Touch home to score a run.
  4. Fair or Foul. Inside the white lines is fair, and a ball on the line counts as fair. A foul ball means you hit again.
  5. Safe or Out. The ball beats you to the base, you get tagged off the base, or your fly ball is caught.
  6. Catch & Throw. Catch fly balls, field grounders and throw to first, and let the closest player get the ball.

Play Ball is a short game that puts it all together. Each team bats until it makes three outs (or scores five runs, a common youth-league limit), then the teams switch.

Controls

Tap or click. With a keyboard: Space swings and continues, 1 2 3 and H pick a base, arrow keys move between buttons, and Esc goes back to the menu.

At the real field

  • Rules vary by league. Many tee ball leagues let every child bat each inning and don't keep score. The game mentions this so it won't be a surprise.
  • Try it in the yard: set out four "bases" and call "first, second, third, home!" as your child runs them in order.
  • A good question after practice: "What do you do after you hit?" The answer is drop the bat and run to first.
