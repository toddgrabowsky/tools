## Retro sports games for young kids

Retro 8-bit games that teach young kids (about age 6) the rules of a sport. Coach Buddy, a cartoon dog, reads every line aloud, so kids who aren't reading yet can play on their own. Each game has six short lessons and a full game against the Dinos, and is a single self-contained HTML file.

- [Super Tee Ball](tee-ball/index.html): tee ball and baseball basics.
- [Super Basketball](basketball/index.html): dribbling, traveling and double dribble, shooting and free throws, defense and fouls, then a 3-on-3 game.
